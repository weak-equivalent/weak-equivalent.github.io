Two instruments, two dyads

Instrument 1: W / S forward / back, A / D steer, Q / E transpose. Instrument 2: ↑ / ↓ forward / back, ← / → steer, , / . transpose. Shift = slow. Mouse: drag the nearer dot on the left.

Window onto the infinite plane; the bright central square is one period (ℝ/12)², faded tiles are periodic copies. x = b, y = a. Filled dot = (b, a) + 12ℤ², hollow = swapped copy (a, b), the same dyad. Shaded: a ≤ b. Solid diagonals: unisons. Dashed: tritones. Arrow: heading.

View:
Right: the quotient (Möbius strip) as the triangle a ≤ b (folded by (a,b) → (min,max)) times time, newest at top. Solid edge: unisons. Dashed legs: glued, (0, y) ~ (y, 12), so a strand leaving one leg re-enters on the other. Drag: free rotation to any angle, wheel: zoom, axis gizmo bottom-left (a′ = lower note, b′ = upper note, t = time). Audio: Shepard tones with separate registers: instrument 1 lower (triangle wave, panned left), instrument 2 higher (sine wave, panned right).
