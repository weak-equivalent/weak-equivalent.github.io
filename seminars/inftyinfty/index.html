(∞,∞)-reading group

Meeting coordinates:
Thursdays 5:30-7:30pm
In person at Chase 319.

We went over the recent paper Oriented Category Theory, by Gepner-Heine, splitting background reading and sections of the paper between participants (note: we used v1 of the arXiv preprint). In most weeks, before our meeting, I would check-in informally with the speaker, and this was productive. There was pizza.

  • Jan 8: overview of (∞,∞)-categories via colimits in PrL. (Theo) MO question, Adrien's notes
  • Jan 15: overview of oriented category theory. (daniel) Adrien's notes
  • Jan 22: on univalent and valent categories. (Scott) speaker notes, Adrien's notes, Daniel's notes
  • Jan 29: on presentability. (Tanner) Adrien's notes
  • Feb 5: on Segal sheaves. (Deni) speaker notes
  • Feb 12: on bienrichment. (Adrien)
  • Feb 26: on Steiner Theory. (Hayato) speaker notes

  • Apr 2: miscellanea on oriented categories. (daniel)
  • Apr 8: informal wrap-up with Naruki Masuda.

Warning: even though this website is public, the reading group is for Dalhousie University students and postdocs. Also, most of the materials were written throughout our learning process, so we make no guarantee that they are correct or complete.
