Dyad space and its braid

Keys: W / S (or ↑ / ↓) drive forward / back along the heading arrow, A / D (or ← / →) steer, Q / E transpose (both notes together), Shift = slow. Mouse: drag the point or either piano marker. Right top: the two notes as strands on the pitch-class circle, time upward. Sound is a Shepard tone, so wrapping at the border is seamless.

Window onto the infinite plane ℝ². The bright central square is one period (ℝ/12)²; faded tiles are periodic copies. x = b, y = a. Filled dots: (b, a) + 12ℤ². Hollow dots: swapped copies (a, b) + 12ℤ², the same dyad. Shaded: a ≤ b. Solid diagonals: unisons. Dashed: tritones. Arrow: heading.
Braid view: drag to rotate. Strands cross exactly at unisons (a = b). Piano: C4–B4, drag a marker.
